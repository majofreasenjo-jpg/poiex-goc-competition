# POIEX / GOC Google Competition Coding Guidance

This project implements a clean-room, competition-specific vertical slice of the Governed Orchestration Core (GOC).

## Constitutional boundary

EL AGENTE PIENSA; EL SISTEMA GOBIERNA.

The ADK/Gemini fleet is advisory. Do not give any LLM agent a material executor tool, Firestore mutation surface, AuthorityLease writer, evidence promoter, target-hash writer, receipt writer, or ControlPlane handle.

A planner may propose. The deterministic GOC boundary owns identity, observed capability, authority, authority provenance, exact target binding, execution, receipts, replay, and promotion of claims.

## V0.8 reasoning constraints

The fleet may recommend a repair, state abstraction, or specialist decomposition, but must not self-certify any of them.

- A route may be ranked by heuristic score, but only deterministic GOC may emit `CERTIFIED_PRUNE`, and only from an exact replayable repair certificate.
- Equal present outputs do not justify merging operational states. If underlying states have rival declared future/action kernels, return `STATE_ALIAS_UNSAFE` or abstain.
- Raw agent/action count and distinct effect-class count are not capacity proofs. Finite-envelope action-capacity observations must not be promoted to unbounded-capacity claims.

Additional negative seals:

- HEURISTIC_BAD_SCORE != CERTIFIED_INFEASIBLE_BRANCH
- PRESENT_EQUIVALENCE != FUTURE_ACTION_KERNEL_EQUIVALENCE
- DISTINCT_ACTION_CLASSES != MULTIPLE_GROWING_CONTROL_DIRECTIONS
- FINITE_ENVELOPE_GROWTH != UNBOUNDED_CAPACITY
